Questions before you book

What do I need to pick up the car?

A driver’s license you have held for at least a year and a credit card in the main driver’s name. Visitors from abroad also need a passport, and an International Driving Permit if the license is not in English.

How old do I need to be?

The main driver must be 21 or older. Additional drivers have to meet the same rules and be present at pick-up.

How do I cancel?

Open , enter your reference and email, and cancel. It’s free until 24 hours before pick-up.

Is there a deposit?

A refundable hold is placed on your credit card at pick-up and released after you return the car undamaged and with a full tank.

Automatic or manual?

Most cars in the US are automatic. Economy cars with a manual gearbox are available at some airports. Write your preference in the booking comment.

Can I return the car at a different airport?

Yes, one-way rentals are possible between most of our airports. Call us to arrange one, since the price depends on the route.
